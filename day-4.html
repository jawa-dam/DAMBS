GEI ACADEMY • DAY 4

The Sluice

Controlled release turns stored water into work.

ENGINEERING STEP 4

The gate to the mill.

Day 4 follows the water from storage toward controlled release. In the GEI model, the sluice or gate regulates the flow that can drive the next stage of the system.

Observe

Look for the transition from stored water to directed movement and controlled flow.

Question

Why would a working hydraulic system need controlled release rather than unrestricted flow?
