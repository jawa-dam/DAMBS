Water & Light

Enter the first stage of the Genesis Engineered Interpretations learning path.

FOUNDATION

Separation begins the system.

Day 1 introduces the GEI reading of the opening movement: waters are present, separation is established, and released water becomes the visual engineering signal represented as light.

Observe

Look for separation, movement and the relationship between heavy water, light water and release.

Question

Ask how the language can be examined as a sequence of physical operations rather than only as abstract description.
